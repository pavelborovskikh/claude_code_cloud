# Henkel: Products, Berlin Tech Hub and AI Systems

Everything below comes from public web sources, searched in November 2026. Where a claim comes only
from Henkel or a vendor, or the source is old, that is stated. Nothing has been filled in that
could not be found.

---

## 1. The company

- **Henkel AG & Co. KGaA**, headquartered in **Düsseldorf, Germany**.
- **2025 sales: about €20.5 billion.** That is 5.1% lower than 2024 in euros, mainly because of
  exchange rates. Excluding currency and acquisition effects, sales grew 0.9%.
- In April 2025 Henkel sold its North American Retailer Brands business. It finished merging its
  consumer businesses into one unit a year ahead of plan.
- Trade reports also name agreed acquisitions of ATP Adhesive Systems, Stahl Group and Not Your
  Mother's. These were not checked against Henkel's own releases.

## 2. Products: two business units

| Unit | 2025 sales | Known brands and products |
|---|---|---|
| **Adhesive Technologies** | about €10.7bn | **Loctite**: industrial adhesives, sealants and coatings, including battery materials for electric vehicles and the new Loctite Specialty Tapes |
| **Consumer Brands** | about €9.7bn | **Persil** (laundry) and **Schwarzkopf** (hair). Its top ten brands made up almost 60% of the unit's sales. Hair care led its 0.3% growth (excluding currency and acquisitions). |

## 3. The Henkel dx Berlin Hub

- **Henkel dx** is Henkel's digital unit, set up in 2020. It combines digital expertise, business
  process management and IT.
- Henkel calls the Berlin hub its **"technical nerve center"**. It builds technology for:
  - selling directly to consumers (D2C)
  - eCommerce
  - connected devices (IoT)
- **Five working areas:**
  - **Experience Center:** for employees and visitors
  - **Digital Garage:** new digital business models and incubating them
  - **Meatpacking District:** where Henkel dx and the business units work together on the RAQN
    platform
  - **Market Hall:** no description of its use was found
  - **Tech Factory:** where Henkel dx developers build RAQN and work on IoT
- **Links to startups:** teams develop new business models with startups, large tech companies and
  mentors. Henkel Ventures invests in young companies in Berlin and elsewhere.
- **The wider network:** Berlin is one of three hubs, alongside **Shanghai** and the **Global
  Technology Center in Bengaluru**.
- **Caveat:** most of these sources date from about 2021 to mid-2024. The hub's current size, staff
  numbers and focus could not be confirmed.

## 4. AI systems built or in progress

| System | What it is | Status and source |
|---|---|---|
| **RAQN digital business platform** | Built in 2021 as the central platform for insights and experiences for consumers and retail partners. It runs on Adobe Experience Platform and gives Henkel one view of business and consumer customers. Part of it is built at the Berlin hub. | Live (Henkel and Adobe) |
| **Generative AI for marketing content (Adobe Firefly)** | Announced March 2024. Henkel can train Firefly on its own assets to create on-brand content. Adobe Journey Optimizer writes message variations automatically for customer journeys. | Announced 2024. Nothing newer was found. |
| **Customer-service AI agents (Cognigy)** | Consumer Brands runs **25 AI agents across 11 countries and 7 channels, serving more than 3 million users**. | Claimed by the vendor Cognigy in its own case study |
| **Loctite Solve** | Launched **April 2026**. An AI-assisted platform for virtual adhesive testing: engineers simulate existing or entirely new adhesives and get material data sheets before any physical prototype exists. Its first focus is EV battery design. It was shown at The Battery Show Europe 2026. | Launched. Claims about shorter development times come from Henkel and haven't been independently checked. |
| **AI-designed adhesive formulas** | Henkel says new formulas proposed by its algorithms succeed **38%** of the time on average, against **8%** for experiments designed by hand. | Henkel's own figure |
| **R&D 4.0 platform** | Combines LabVantage's lab data system with SAP's product lifecycle tools and moves R&D data to the cloud, to support technologies such as generative AI. | Reported as in progress |
| **Trumbull (USA) R&D center** | A merged North American R&D center with testing robots driven by machine learning and digital twins. The aim is to predict how products in hair, skin, fabric and surface care will end up performing. | Planned or being built (trade press) |
| **R&D data in a large language model** | Henkel's Chief Digital and Information Officer (CDIO) described a plan to put all consumer-goods R&D data into an LLM to speed up product development. | A stated plan. No confirmed outcome was found. |
| **Internal data assistant in Microsoft Teams** | An assistant inside Teams that answers employees' questions by running database queries. It is described as an always-available digital colleague for regional teams. | Described only in CDO Magazine. No official launch, date or usage figures were found. |

- **Leadership:** the CDIO is **Michael Nilles**. He describes generative AI as central to Henkel's
  digital transformation and calls "massive upskilling" of employees a priority.
- **Not found:** an official launch of a company-wide generative AI tool for employees, or a
  confirmed Microsoft generative AI partnership.
